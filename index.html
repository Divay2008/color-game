Chroma City

The grumpy gray Murks have drained the color from Chroma City: every building and house is gray! Help Professor Prism fetch the right colors from the Color Wheel Plaza and repaint the whole city.

  1. Look at the glowing orb on the Professor's staff and the marker on the arena rim.
  2. Read the rule at the top (like Complementary) and run to the orb that follows it.
  3. Each orb stands on its own hue, so the floor wheel shows you the answer.
  4. Every character has a different job in the city: painter, gardener, lamplighter, mechanic or balloon man. Each right answer gives you supplies. Press E to do your job, and pick another character to try another job. There is no time limit.
  5. Every right answer also paints buildings and houses in a matching color harmony. Murk hits fade them back to gray. Collect gold coins around the city for bonus points.
  6. Only 5 gold coins lie on the color wheel. The rest are hidden around the city, tucked beside buildings, houses and parks. Spend coins in the Coin Shop (press B): hints, hearts, job supplies, Rainbow Dash and a coin radar. Press Esc or Exit to leave a level.
  7. Grab golden stars for Rainbow Dash: Murks cheer up and you score points by catching them.
  8. Jump over Murks, and dance to make the crowd cheer!
  9. Clear all 13 levels: tints, shades, tones and hue families first, then the color harmonies. Press L any time to open the Color Lab. You get 5 hearts per level.
Choose your character

Move: WASD or arrows. Do your job: E. Jump: Space. Dance: Q. On a phone, drag to steer and use the round buttons.

Choose a level

Finish a level to unlock the next. Keep your hearts to earn 3 stars.

Leave the level?

Your progress in this level will be lost. Stars and coins you already earned are kept.

Color Lab

Every color has a hue (its color family), a saturation (how vivid it is) and a lightness. Mix it with white, black or gray to make tints, shades and tones.

Shades hue + black
